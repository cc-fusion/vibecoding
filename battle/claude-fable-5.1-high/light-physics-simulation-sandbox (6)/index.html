Rays are traced spectrally (380–740 nm). Prisms use Snell's law with Cauchy dispersion and Fresnel coefficients; gratings use the grating equation with a single-slit envelope; polarization is tracked with Stokes parameters (0° = in-plane). Sensors report absorbed power in the source's power units.
