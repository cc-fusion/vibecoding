How to save a reef

1-minute start
  1. Pick a reef, press LAUNCH.
  2. Select Paint MPA (2), drag over a coral patch.
  3. Press ▶ Play. Fish shelter in the MPA and graze algae.
  4. When algae spreads, restock fish / release urchins.
  5. During a heatwave, watch for white bleached coral — it can recover when water cools.
The 4 linked systems
  • Coral ⇄ Algae: compete for space. Nutrients & dead coral favour algae.
  • Grazers: parrotfish + urchins eat algae. Overfishing removes them.
  • Predators & COTS: groupers eat grazers; crown-of-thorns eat coral.
  • Climate & humans: SST, pH, fishing, pollution, MPAs & budget all interact.
