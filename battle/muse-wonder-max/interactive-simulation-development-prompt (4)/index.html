    CONTROLLER HANDBOOK

    Everything you need in your first minute — and the depth after it.

    The 60-second loop

    1. Click a cyan arrival blip (or its traffic row).
    2. Press Direct Final — it turns toward the FAF fix, west of the field.
    3. Slow it: speed ≤ 200 kt, altitude ≤ 4,000 ft before the FAF.
    4. Press Clear to Land when the runway is clear. +100 pts.
    5. Departures: pick one in the queue → Clear Takeoff → vector it to its named exit gate.

    Separation & safety

    Keep aircraft 3 NM apart horizontally or 1,000 ft apart vertically. Amber/red rings mean a conflict is developing — change altitude first, it is the fastest fix. A loss of separation costs safety and points; a collision ends the shift instantly. Heavy jets leave wake turbulence: wait a few seconds before landing or launching traffic behind them.

    Weather, wind & fuel

    Orange storm cells drift with the wind. Flying through one causes turbulence and extra fuel burn; a cell over the airport closes the runway and forces go-arounds. Holds buy spacing but burn fuel fast — below 12 minutes of fuel, land that aircraft first or declare priority. Wind shifts change your headwind: strong tailwinds block landings.

    Winning & losing

    Meet the landing + departure quotas before the shift clock runs out with safety ≥ 60 to clear the shift and unlock the next. You fail instantly on a mid-air collision or a fuel-exhaustion crash, and at the buzzer if quotas are missed. Emergencies (red) are worth double — but never at the cost of separation.
