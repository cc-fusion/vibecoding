How the Lab Works

THE CORE LOOP

Everything is made of nodes joined by spring members, simulated with Verlet integration. Build or spawn a structure, press play, watch stress flow through it as colour (green → amber → red), then reinforce, cut or re-pin and test again. Members snap when strain passes their material limit.

FIVE LINKED SYSTEMS

  • Structures — cloth, ropes, beams and rigid frames share one solver; loading one part strains its neighbours.
  • Pressure bodies — blobs hold gas pressure: raise pressure and they stiffen and push harder on whatever they touch; puncture one and it leaks and sags.
  • Environment — gravity, wind gusts and water buoyancy/drag all feed forces into the same nodes.
  • Materials & budget — wood is cheap and weak, steel and carbon (unlocked by passing tests) carry far more strain per credit.
  • Damage — snapped members redistribute load, so one failure can cascade. Integrity and peak stress chart it live.

TOOLS (keys 1–0)

Grab drag nodes (throw them!). Pin toggles anchors; clicking empty space plants an anchor node. Cut slices members you drag across. Erase deletes nodes. Cloth / Rope / Blob / Crate spawn by click-dragging a size. Beam drags a structural member between snapped nodes. Weight drops a heavy ball.

CONTROLS

P pause · M mute · H help · Ctrl+Z undo · Space scenario action (start test / attach crate) · Crane: AD trolley, WS winch · Speed keys , .

THE THREE TESTS

Bridge Under Load — reinforce the rope deck in build phase, then send the heavy cart across. Crane Lift — drive the trolley, winch the hook, attach the crate and set it in the target zone gently. Shake Tower — brace the tower, then survive 30 s of rising earthquake. Passing tests earns stars, XP and stronger materials. Fail any test by letting the load fall, the crate smash, or the tower collapse.
