How to Steward a Commons

The core loop (60 seconds)

  1. Press play and watch boats fish and stocks move.
  2. Click sea tiles — protect the densest nursery areas as MPAs.
  3. Trim any quota glowing red (above advice while stock falls).
  4. Watch prices, fleet profits and the harbour log react next month.
  5. Spend science on Selective Gear first in most runs.

System 1 · Biology

Each stock grows logistically per-tile, recruits in spring, diffuses between tiles and spills out of MPAs. Warming water helps tuna, hurts cod. Bycatch of juveniles quietly steals future adults. A stock under 3% of virgin biomass for 3 months is extinct — game over.

System 2 · Fleets & Market

Community, Industrial and Artisan fleets chase profit: price up or fuel down means more effort. Legal fleets share your TAC; the Industrial fleet cheats when enforcement is thin, and foreign poachers arrive when prices are high and patrols are low. Scarcity raises prices — which raises effort. That loop is the tragedy.

System 3 · Treasury & Port

Landing taxes, licence fees and poacher fines fund enforcement, MPAs and festivals. The port's jobs track landings — starve the fleet and approval collapses; bankrupt the treasury (−$400k) and your mandate ends.

System 4 · Research

Selective gear cuts bycatch, satellites cheapen enforcement, restored habitat raises carrying capacity, aquaculture decouples port jobs from wild catch. Research is how a hard scenario becomes winnable.

Winning & losing

Win early: all stocks ≥65%, ecology ≥70, port ≥55 for 12 straight months.
Win at term end: ecology ≥55, weakest stock ≥35%, port ≥45, treasury ≥0.
Lose: any extinction, bankruptcy, or approval under 10% for a full year.
