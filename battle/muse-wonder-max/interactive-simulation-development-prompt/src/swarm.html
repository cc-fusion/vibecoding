How the Lab Works

Goal loop (60 seconds): pick Food tool → click field to feed → drag Separation/Alignment sliders → watch order Φ and spacing react → dodge the hawk with a Scare burst.

Systems: ① Flocking rules create order/clusters. ② Energy falls with speed & turning, rises at food/roosts — tired agents slow down, changing the flock shape. ③ Hawks learn and hunt stragglers, so spacing trades safety vs. efficiency. ④ Wind & gusts push everyone, breaking/forming streams. ⑤ Food depletes and blooms move the flock. In PSO mode the same agents become optimizer particles pulled by personal/global bests. In Sheepdog mode your dog is a moving predator the sheep flock away from — position behind them relative to the pen.

Tools: Look, Food (golden = rich), Rock, Roost, Scare, Erase. Mouse/touch drag paints food/rocks. WASD/arrows steer the dog (or your hawk with Predator tool selected in free modes). Space pause, 1–6 tools, +/- speed, M mute, H help.

Winning: complete each scenario objective before its clock ends. Fail by losing too much of the flock or running out of time. Free Murmuration is an endless sandbox — press Finish/Evaluate after holding Φ ≥ 0.85 for 5 s to earn its badge.
