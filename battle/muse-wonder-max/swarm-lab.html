How Swarm Lab works

🐦 The three rules
Separation avoids crowding, Alignment matches neighbours' heading, Cohesion pulls toward the local centre. Drag the sliders live — order (φ) collapses or emerges in seconds. Too much cohesion + no separation = a knot. Too much separation = confetti.
🦅 Predators hunt stragglers
Hawks target the most isolated agent — exactly like real starling murmurations. Tight flocks are safer but drain energy faster when they sprint. Tired birds must land on a ⌂ roost to recover.
⬢ Forage (ACO)
Ants deposit food-pheromone on the way home and home-pheromone on the way out. Trails evaporate — humidity (turbulence) speeds decay. Shortest paths win because they get reinforced fastest.
◎ Optimize (PSO)
Each particle remembers its personal best and is pulled toward the swarm's global best. Inertia too high = overshoot forever. Find the amber peak before time runs out.
🐑 Sheepdog capstone
Sheep flee the dog, flock to each other, and drift to graze. Position the dog behind the flock relative to the pen — pushing from the wrong side scatters them. WASD or drag. Get all sheep in the green pen before the timer ends.

Space pause · 1–7 tools · WASD/Arrows steer · G gust · F food · P predator · M mute · H help · Esc menu. Touch: drag to steer, tap tools then tap canvas.
