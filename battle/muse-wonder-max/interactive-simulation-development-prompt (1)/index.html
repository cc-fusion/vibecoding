    How to run this desk

    Goal. Grow your equity to the scenario target before the closing bell — without getting margin-liquidated. Each scenario changes volatility, news flow and the hidden fundamental trend.

    The book. Green bids / red asks in the centre are real resting orders from 58 agents. Market orders cross the spread instantly (paying spread + slippage + fees). Limit orders rest in the book and may earn the spread — click the chart to set a limit price.

    Read the market. Price chases a hidden fundamental value (dashed line, shown as a noisy estimate). Momentum agents chase trends, mean-reverters fade extremes, informed traders already know the fundamental, HFTs pick off stale quotes. Watch depth imbalance and the tape to see who is winning.

    Risk. Your margin bar shows gross exposure ÷ equity. Past 100% of your leverage you cannot add. Below maintenance you get a margin call; below liquidation the desk force-closes you and the day is failed. Stop-loss / take-profit fields auto-flatten you.

    Bot & upgrades. Build an algo (strategy, threshold, size, cap) that trades your account, and spend ◆ EDGE — earned from profitable closes and milestones — on execution speed, a sharper fundamental feed, wider margin and auto-risk shields.

    Chaos systems. Volatility clusters (calm breeds calm, shocks breed shocks), market makers withdraw in panics, and a >6% move in 30 minutes trips the circuit breaker. The Stress-Test Director lets you inject news, whales and liquidity drains to learn the cascades.
