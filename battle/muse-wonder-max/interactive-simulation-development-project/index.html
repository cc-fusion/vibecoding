How the Lab works

The 60-second version: press Enter the Lab, then drag Alignment up and watch scattered dots snap into a single flowing ribbon — that's emergence. Then hit Release Hawk and watch it shatter. Your job in every experiment: keep the swarm coherent, fed and alive.

Flocking ⇄ Everything
Separation / alignment / cohesion shape order φ. High φ scores points in Exp. 1, keeps fish safe in packs, and makes sheep predictable for your dog.
Energy ⇄ Speed ⇄ Food
Flying burns energy; slow, tired agents can't flee or feed. Food blooms and roosts refill them. Starving swarms dissolve.
Predators ⇄ Order
Hawks chase the nearest agent. Fleeing breaks alignment — but tight, fast flocks out-turn a hawk. Every catch costs population and score.
Wind & rocks ⇄ Flow
Wind pushes light bees hardest, starlings least. Rocks split schools (nearest-neighbour distance spikes) — sometimes that's exactly what a dog needs.

Experiments: ① Hold φ ≥ 0.80 for 12 s. ② Eat 35 food before time runs out. ③ Survive 120 s of hawks with ≥ 25 alive. ④ PSO: particles remember personal bests and follow the global best across a hidden landscape — find fitness 92+. ⑤ Capstone: steer the dog (mouse or WASD, B to bark) and pen 12 sheep.

Tools: Inspect (follow one agent), Food, Rock, Roost, Wind (drag on the world to aim a gust), Erase. Sliders act instantly — experiment mid-crisis. Speed controls (0.5×–4×) sit top-right; Space pauses.

Research points from every run raise your Lab Level and persist between visits, along with campaign stars, unlocked experiments and your audio/ graphics settings.
