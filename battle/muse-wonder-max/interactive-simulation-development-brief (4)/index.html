How to Play — The Geneticist's Handbook

Understandable in a minute, deep for hours.

1 · The core loop

Inspect animals → set a Sire and Dam → read the Punnett / target forecast → Breed → pups mature in ~6 days → keep, contract-deliver, or release the rest. Every litter costs credits and every animal eats daily.

2 · Dominance (A, D)

A Agouti hides a black. But dd at the D locus is epistatic: it switches pigment off entirely and the animal is Albino no matter what A says. Carriers look normal — genotype is destiny you cannot always see.

3 · Codominance & ears (B, Ear)

BS/BT heterozygotes are visibly Patchwork (spots + stripes). Ear U/F heterozygotes are Wavy — one up, one floppy. Heterozygotes are not hidden here; they are a third phenotype.

4 · Sex-linkage & linkage

Ruby eye sits on the X. Males (XY) express their single allele; females need r/r. Meanwhile A and B sit 18 cM apart on chromosome 1, so parental haplotypes mostly travel together — recombination (18%) creates the rare new combos.

5 · Polygenic size

Three additive loci (P1–P3) give 0–6 “+” growth alleles. Size = genes + a little lifelong environmental noise. Response to selection follows R = h²S: pick the biggest parents, but the biggest are often related…

6 · Inbreeding depression

Offspring F is computed from shared ancestors. High F cuts health, litter size and lifespan, and raises pup mortality. Outcross, import champions, and accept wandering carriers to rescue a line.

7 · Economy & drift

Small populations lose alleles by drift even with perfect intentions. Contracts and releases fund breeding; upgrades (habitat, incubator, nutrition, scanner, cryobank) are permanent across the campaign and cost Research Points from milestones.

8 · Winning & losing

Produce the target phenotype/genotype in a lab-born animal before the generation and day limits. You lose if time runs out, credits fall below −50, or no breeding population remains.
