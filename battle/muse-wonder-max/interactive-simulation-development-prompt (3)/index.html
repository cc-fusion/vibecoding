    COMMANDER'S HANDBOOK

    Goal. Reach your scenario's target Sol with every crew member alive, survive the announced capstone dust storm, and hit the required self-sufficiency by the final evaluation (30-sol grace period after target Sol).

    Air. Crew consume O₂ and exhale CO₂. Electrolysis turns water into O₂ + hydrogen. The Sabatier reactor combines stored CO₂ + hydrogen back into water — closing the loop. Scrubbers pull CO₂ from cabin air. Crops add O₂ while lit. CO₂ above ~2,500 ppm headaches the crew; above 4,000 it injures them. O₂ partial pressure below 12 kPa is an emergency.

    Water. Crew and crops consume clean water; the recycler recovers most wastewater (efficiency rises with its slider, power and health). Electrolysis is a net water sink unless Sabatier runs. Ice-mining EVAs are your renewable source.

    Power. Solar peaks at midday and collapses under dust opacity and panel soiling — clean panels by EVA or unlock dust-repellent coating. The RTG gives constant baseline power. Batteries bridge the night; if demand exceeds supply + battery, everything browns out proportionally, heaters included.

    Heat. Mars swings from −20°C by day to −80°C at night. Heater power and waste heat keep the cabin near 21°C. Cold cabins stress crew, stall crops and thicken pump wear.

    Food & crops. Plant in the greenhouse, keep lights, water, warmth and CO₂ healthy, then harvest. Botanists accelerate growth. Harvested-versus-consumed food is one third of self-sufficiency, alongside water recovery and O₂ closure.

    Crew. Assign roles: Engineers slow wear and speed repairs, Botanists grow crops, Scientists earn Research Points and Mission Support, Medics heal, EVA Specialists make sorties safer, Rest restores energy and lowers stress. Exhausted, hungry, frightened crew get sick — and a single death fails the mission.

    Wear & failure. Every running component wears. Below 25% health it may fail outright, cascading (a dead recycler quietly becomes a water crisis). Repair with spares before it breaks.

    Progression. Spend Research Points on six technologies. Earn Mission Support from science and milestones, spend it during 3-sol resupply windows every 45 sols. Completing scenarios earns persistent commendations on the title screen.

    Controls. Mouse/touch for everything; click canvas modules to inspect. Space pause, 1–4 speeds 0.5/1/2/4×, H help, M mute, S save. The sim auto-pauses when the tab loses focus.
