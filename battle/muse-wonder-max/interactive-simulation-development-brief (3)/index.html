    How to Play — Spiking Neural Lab

    The living circuit

    Each dot is an Izhikevich neuron (v: membrane voltage, u: recovery). Excitatory RS cells excite, FS inhibitory cells suppress. Spikes travel along synapses; brightness shows voltage.

    STDP plasticity
    “Cells that fire together wire together.” If a presynaptic cell fires just before its target, that synapse strengthens; reverse order weakens it. Teach patterns by co-stimulating groups, then test with a partial cue.

    Balance or break

    Healthy networks fire ~5–30 Hz with moderate synchrony. Too much drive / too little inhibition → seizure. Too much inhibition / decay → silence. Both drain Lab Integrity — at 0 the experiment fails.

    Your levers interact
    Drive & noise feed activity → activity drives STDP → STDP reshapes rhythm → topology decides where rhythm can travel. Change one, watch them all.

    Tools

    Inspect (trace a cell), Stimulate ⚡, Inhibit 🧊, place pulsing Electrodes 📍, Lesion ✂ (click again to restore). Sliders retune E/I balance live — no rebuild needed.

    Missions

    1 Awaken → keep a stable firing band. 2 Pattern Memory → teach & recall. 3 CPG → alternating module rhythm. 4 Classifier Gauntlet (capstone) → train X/Y decisions, pass 4 of 5 blind trials. Sandbox is endless.
