RUN STATISTICS

FIELD MANUAL

Your job: keep the town and farms alive until the season clock runs out, on budget, without stripping the watershed bare.

The five coupled systems
  • Terrain & thermal erosion — steep bare slopes slump downhill until they reach a stable angle. Valleys incise over time.
  • Water — rain and river inflow pond, flow downhill, evaporate and infiltrate. Saturated soil infiltrates less, so wet watersheds flood faster.
  • Hydraulic erosion & sediment — fast water picks up soil (worse on bare ground), carries it, and drops it where flow slows: lakes, floodplains, behind dams. Deposition can choke channels and raise flood levels.
  • Groundwater & vegetation — moisture feeds plants; roots cut erosion by up to ~80% and slow runoff. Drowning, drought and erosion kill plants. Forests spread on moist fertile soil.
  • People & structures — farms earn harvest income on flat, moist, fertile, unflooded land. Flood depth damages farms and the town. Dams store flood peaks but can overtop and breach; levees protect locally but push water elsewhere; terraces flatten and armour slopes.
Tools — Inspect, Raise Land, Dig Channel, Rain Seeder, Plant Forest, Dam, Levee, Terrace, Plant Farm, Repair, Demolish. Every earthwork costs money per cell; structures also cost daily maintenance. Watch the tool card for exact costs.
Events — storms, sediment surges, droughts, landslides on saturated slopes, dam stress warnings, and (in the capstone) a 100-year megaflood. The forecast panel warns you — build before the red banner, not during it.
Winning & losing — survive to the final day with all objectives met to clear the scenario and unlock the next. You fail early if the town is destroyed, every farm is lost, the watershed's vegetation collapses, or debt passes −$400.
