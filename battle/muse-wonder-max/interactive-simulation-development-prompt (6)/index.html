    How to Steward a Forest

    The core loop

    Time flows in years (watch seasons sweep the map). Trees grow, compete, seed new saplings and die — opening gaps. Plant species where they fit, harvest mature timber for income, thin crowded thickets, and keep canopy cover, carbon and biodiversity climbing toward your scenario goals before the deadline year.

    Four systems that talk to each other

    • Light: each tree's growth depends on how much taller neighbours shade it. Pioneers (birch) need full sun; maple & spruce tolerate deep shade and inherit the forest floor.
    • Soil: every cell holds moisture & nutrients. Roots drain them, rain and leaf-litter refill them. Willow collapses in drought; pine shrugs it off.
    • Wildlife: deer multiply on sapling forage and browse your regeneration to death unless wolves (Wildlife Corridors research, or old-growth habitat) check them. Birds in old trees carry seeds further.
    • Disturbance: fire races through dry conifers but stops at firebreaks and wet willow stands; beetles spread tree-to-tree through spruce & pine; windthrow fells the tallest. Each resets succession — and pays out in nutrient-rich ash and light.

    Money & knowledge

    Harvesting mature trees pays by species and size (oak is precious, willow is pulp). Each year you receive an ecosystem-services grant scaled by canopy cover and biodiversity, plus carbon credits for net new carbon stored. Knowledge (violet flask) accrues from a diverse, monitored forest and buys permanent research. Drop below −$2,000 and the trust is bankrupt — game over.

    Controls

    Space pause/resume · 1–8 select tool · [] brush size · +− speed · M mute · H help · Esc pause menu. Mouse: click/drag on the map with the active tool; works with touch too. Hover any species card or stat for explanations.

    Winning & losing

    Meet all scenario goals before the deadline to win (you may keep playing in sandbox mode). You lose if the trust goes bankrupt, canopy cover stays under 4% for 3 straight years (ecological collapse), the deadline passes with goals unmet — or, in Ember Frontier, the town burns.
