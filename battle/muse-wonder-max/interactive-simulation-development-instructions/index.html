How Swarm Lab Works

Understand it in a minute. Master it over a campaign.

The 3 Rules (Boids)

Separation steers away from crowding neighbours. Alignment matches their average heading. Cohesion pulls toward their centre. No agent sees the whole flock — murmurations, schools and traffic-jam waves emerge. Order Φ (0–1) measures polarisation.

Energy, Food & Evolution

Flying burns energy; low energy slows agents. Food restores it — well-fed agents may reproduce, passing on speed & vision genes with 4% mutation. Roosts (♣) let agents rest safely. Generations tick up as offspring are born.

Predators Learn

Hawks don't chase the flock centre — they target the straggler (largest nearest-neighbour distance), exactly like real predators. Tighten cohesion to protect loners, or scatter (spacebar tools) to confuse the strike. Caught agents burst into feather particles.

Wind, Obstacles & Pheromones

Wind pushes every agent and drifts food scent. Rocks force flow-splitting. Foragers lay a fading pheromone field (toggle it on) toward food — trails strengthen when food is found, like ants. Gusts (G) can rescue or ruin a formation.

PSO Mode — Living Optimisation

Particles remember their personal best and share a global best — particle swarm optimisation. Watch them explore the Ackley / Rastrigin / Himmelblau landscapes, escape local minima, and converge on the glowing optimum. Your flocking sliders become inertia & social weights.

Capstone: Sheepdog Trial

You are the dog. Sheep flee you but flock together — approach wide, balance the flock, and push at least 80% into the green pen before the clock ends. Two hawks circle in Chaotic. This is unlocked by earning campaign stars.
