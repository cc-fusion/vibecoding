A field guide to the North Reach

Keeper's notes

Four boats cross each night. Your beam is a compass, a welcome, and a promise that the rocks are not where they seem.

Read the sail

Trade boats need the wide beam. Fishing skiffs need focus. Keep your light on them until their guide bar fills, then turn to the next boat.

Mind the lens

Focus burns oil faster and warms the lens. Bank the light to refill oil and cool it. An overheated lens will go dark for a few seconds.

Answer a distress

Point toward an SOS boat and fire a flare. A flare makes its passage much quicker, but a patient wide beam can still guide it without one.

Read the weather

Fog lengthens a safe passage. Gales hurry boats and drink more oil. Successful rescues return a little oil and brass for lasting improvements.
