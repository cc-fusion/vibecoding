How to play

Add elements from the left rail. Drag bodies to move, drag the small ring to rotate. Click empty space to deselect. Del removes selection.

Flashlights emit a diverging cone with a paintable spectrum. Lasers are single-wavelength and polarized. Everything else uses real optics:

  • · Snell n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂ + Fresnel Rₛ,Rₚ at every interface
  • · Sellmeier dispersion n(λ) for prisms, lenses and glass
  • · Grating equation d(sinθₘ − sinθᵢ) = mλ, d = 1/lines-per-mm
  • · Thin-lens deflection Δθ ≈ −h/f with chromatic f(λ)
  • · Malus I = I₀cos²θ + Beer–Lambert T = e^(−αL)
  • · Planck black-body for sunlight / tungsten presets

Try: white flashlight → prism → blocker screen. Or laser → polarizer → polarizer crossed at 90°.
