Physics used (no fakes)
  • Snell + Fresnel — n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂, Rₛ/Rₚ from full Fresnel equations, TIR when sinθ > n₂/n₁
  • Sellmeier dispersion — n(λ)² = 1 + Σ Bᵢλ²/(λ²−Cᵢ) for BK7 / SF10 / silica (lens focus + prism rainbow shift with λ)
  • Grating equation — sinθₘ = sinθᵢₙ + mλ/d, d = 1/lines-per-mm, orders |sin| ≤ 1
  • Thin-lens — θₒᵤₜ = θᵢₙ − atan(h/f(λ)), f(λ) scaled by (n_d−1)/(n(λ)−1)
  • Malus + Stokes-ish — tracks in-plane / out-of-plane power, I = Iₚcos²A + Iₛsin²A
  • Planck blackbody — sunlight / tungsten spectra from Planck's law; LED = gaussians
  • Curved mirror — exact circle intersection, f = R/2
Tip: white LED through a 600 lines/mm transmission grating or an SF10 prism gives the cleanest rainbow. Cross two polarizers at 0° / 90° to kill any beam.
