How to Play

Goal

Zombies walk from the right toward your house on the left. Plant defenses on the 5×9 lawn to stop every wave. If a zombie reaches a lawn mower it gets run over — but each mower only works once. If a zombie gets past, the zombies eat your brains!

Sun economy

Click falling sun to collect it. Sunflowers & Sun-shrooms produce more. Plants cost sun and seed packets recharge after use. At night no sun falls — rely on cheap mushrooms. Mushrooms sleep during the day unless woken by a Coffee Bean.

Lawn variants

Pool: the two middle lanes are water; place a Lily Pad first (or an aquatic plant). Fog: the right side is hidden; Planterns reveal it. Conveyor: no sun — plants arrive on a belt. Boss: Dr. Zomboss blocks the last two columns; shoot his Zombot, extinguish fireballs with Snow Peas.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch click a seed packet, then click a tile to plant. Click sun & coins to collect.
  • 1–8 select seed packet · S shovel · Right-click/Esc cancel selection
  • ←↑↓→ move tile cursor · Enter/Space plant / dig at cursor
  • P or Esc pause · M mute

Tips

  • Two Sunflower columns early beats one Peashooter.
  • Pole Vaulters & Dolphins jump the first plant — use Tall-nuts or bait.
  • Screen Doors block peas but not Fume-shroom fumes or Spikeweed.
  • Torchwood doubles damage of peas passing through. Fire peas thaw Snow Pea slow — don't mix in one lane.
  • Chompers eat anything in one bite, even Footballs, then chew for a while.
  • Upgrades in the shop are permanent. Earn coins in the Zen Garden between levels.
