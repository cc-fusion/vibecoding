The Job

Every heist: get in, find the vault, steal the target, and get back out to the street. Everything else you pocket on the way is profit for the workshop.

Sight & Light

Automatons see in the glowing cone ahead of them. Their eyes fill a detection meter — slower at long range, in darkness, or while you sneak; instantly if you brush past their lens. Gas lamps light you up: snuff them (E) to shrink enemy sight by almost half. A patrol that spots a dark lamp will go relight it.

Sound

Running makes noise depending on the floor: carpet is quiet, stone moderate, wood creaks, metal grating rings out. Sneaking is silent. Hounds hear twice as far. Guards walk toward sounds — toss a coin to send them the wrong way.

The Chronometer

Hold Space to slow the world to a fifth of its pace while you move freely. It drains in real seconds; refill it by stealing time: takedowns, pocket watches and wall clocks. Chrono-Wardens are immune.

Takedowns & Bodies

Approach an unalerted automaton from behind and press E. Hounds can be taken from any side; Brass Knights and Watchers only after being jammed. A patrol that finds a fallen comrade raises the alarm — salvage bodies (E) for scrap gold to erase the evidence.

Alarm

A guard who sees you clearly shouts after a moment — silence it first! Each alarm raises the bell level: faster, sharper guards, then reinforcements at the entrance, then full lockdown. It cools down when the watch loses you.

Loot & Weight

Coins, gems and watches weigh nothing. Candlesticks, gold bars and paintings fill your satchel and slow you down. Safes hold the best gems but need picking.

Hazards

Pressure plates ring the alarm when stepped on. Steam vents scald on a cycle — but their steam also hides you. Aether beams trip the alarm unless you sneak under them.

Ratings

Ghost (never spotted, no alarm): +50% gold. Shadow (no alarm): +25%. Finish under par time for a speed bonus. Captured thieves lose 70% of the night's take.
