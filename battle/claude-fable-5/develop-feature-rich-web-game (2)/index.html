Field Manual

Goal

Lead the Azure warband through 6 battles across the folded lands and topple the Crimson King. Earn gold, recruit troops, and enact decrees between battles.

How a turn works

Click a blue unit → blue tiles show where it can move, red fringe shows its reach. Click a tile to move, then click a red enemy in range to strike, or press W / Wait. Units that acted turn grey. Press Enter or End Turn when done — then the Crimson host moves.

Terrain is everything

🌲 Forest+2 defense, costs 2 move (3 for knights)
⛰ Mountain+3 defense, +1 attack, ranged +1 reach. Costs 3 move. Knights can't climb.
⬆ Hill+1 defense, +1 attack, ranged +1 reach. Costs 2 move.
〰 RiverImpassable — cross at bridges.

The troops

⚔ SoldierSteady line infantry. Counters at melee.
🏹 ArcherStrikes at range 2 (3 from high ground) but can't fight adjacent — screen them!
🐎 KnightFast and brutal, but weak in woods and blocked by peaks.
🔮 MageFire that ignores armor, range 1–2. Fragile.
✚ ClericClick a wounded ally beside them to heal 8. Weak in a fight.

Combat

Damage = attack − defense (terrain adds defense). Defenders counter-attack if you're within their reach — archers can't counter adjacent foes, soldiers can't counter arrows. Mages ignore armor.

Controls

Mouse / Touch select, move, attack   Enter end turn   W wait   Tab next unit   Esc deselect / pause   H help   M mute
