🎯 GOAL

Fill the QUOTA of orders before the timer ends. Missed orders cost ❤️. Lose all hearts = shift failed!

🔄 BAKE LOOP

  1. Grab ingredients at dispensers (E)
  2. Drop them in the Mixer → wait for dough (⏳ = add more for bigger recipes!)
  3. Load dough into Oven → take out when GOLDEN (not burnt!)
  4. If needed, finish at Glazer with topping
  5. Deliver at DROP-OFF pad!

🌀 ZERO-G TIPS

  • You drift! Tap thrust, don't hold. Dash (Space) for bursts.
  • Q throws held items — they float! Catch with E.
  • Thrown items can be caught by your partner across the kitchen!
  • Dash through crumbs to clean. Dash into mites to bonk them!
