Worldkeeper's Manual

Understandable in 60 seconds · Mastered in 60 seasons

CORE LOOP
Seed plants → herbivores graze & breed → carnivores hunt → scavengers & fungi recycle corpses into soil → richer soil grows more plants. Watch the census; intervene before collapse.
WATER & SOIL
Rain & irrigation raise tile moisture. Plants drink it. Decomposers (shrooms) restore fertility where things die. Overlays (right panel) reveal both hidden fields.
GENES EVOLVE
Every birth mutates speed · size · vision · metabolism. Famine selects the efficient; predators select the fast. Track mean speed in Charts.
CRISES
Drought, plague, blizzards strike harder on harsh worlds. The Apex Maw invasion is the capstone — keep 5+ species & 40+ animals alive through it.
GOD TOOLS (keys 1–8)
1 Inspect · 2 Raise land · 3 Lower land · 4 Seed flora (2✦) · 5 Add grazer (6✦) · 6 Add fox (10✦) · 7 Irrigate · 8 Lightning cull. R = rain, X = plague.
WIN / LOSE
WIN: reach Year 8 with ≥5 thriving species, ≥40 fauna, and survive an Apex invasion. LOSE: all fauna extinct for 30s (collapse). Extinct species can re-evolve via seeding.
