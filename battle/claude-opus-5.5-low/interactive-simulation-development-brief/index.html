How the Biosphere Works

Controls

  • Right-drag (or left-drag with 🎥) orbit · Wheel/R/F zoom · Middle-drag / WASD pan · Q/E rotate
  • Left-click/drag applies the selected tool
  • Touch: one finger = tool (or orbit with 🎥), two fingers = pinch-zoom & pan
  • Space pause · 1–5 speed · Esc menu · H help · M mute
  • Tools: C camera, I inspect, T raise, G lower, P seed, U water, B spawn, X cull, V disease
  • Spawn / cull / disease act on the species selected in the 🐾 tab

Systems

  • Climate: day–night, 4 seasons (5 days each), weather, altitude cooling & climate dial set temperature.
  • Soil & water: moisture comes from rain and lakes. Fertility gets used up by plant growth and comes back as detritus decays.
  • Plants: grow with warmth, water, fertility and light, and spread seeds to neighbouring cells. Flowers boost seed output, but only when bees pollinate them.
  • Animals: driven by hunger, thirst, rest and reproduction. Offspring inherit mutated genes (speed, vision, size, cold tolerance, longevity). Faster and bigger animals burn more food.
  • Nutrient loop: deaths leave carcasses → crows & beetles → detritus → fertility → plants.

Progression

Every hour you earn ✨ Insight, more when the ecosystem is diverse (Shannon index). Spend it on tools and on 🔬 Research: wider terraforming, the climate dial, weather control, a pathogen lab, rangers and more. Winning scenarios unlocks harder ones. Milestones are saved permanently.

Win & Lose

Win: reach the scenario's final day with at least 4 of the 5 native species alive and fewer than 3 dire wolves left.
Lose: 3 or more native species extinct at the same time, or plant cover drops below 2% (barren world).

Tips

  • Too many hares strip the land bare; foxes keep them in check.
  • Bees die off without flowers, and plants slow down without bees.
  • Wildfires spread through dry vegetation. Water or rain puts them out.
  • Use 🔍 to tag an animal and follow its life.
