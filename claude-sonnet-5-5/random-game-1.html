How to play
  • Pick a tower (1-7) and click a free tile to build. Enemies follow the glowing road to your base; every leak costs lives.
  • Click a tower to upgrade (U), sell (S) or change its targeting (T). Level 3 unlocks a special ability.
  • Amplifiers boost nearby towers. Cryo slows, Railguns pierce armor, Tesla chains, Mortars splash, Lasers ramp up.
  • Start waves with Space. Clearing a wave pays a bonus plus interest on your savings.
  • Abilities: Q Orbital Strike (click to aim), W Cryo Blast.
  • Survive wave 30 (Overlord) to win. Bosses arrive at waves 10, 20 and 30; mini-bosses at 5, 15, 25.
  • F speed · P pause · Esc/right-click cancel · M mute
