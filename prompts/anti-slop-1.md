When designing the application, avoid content that looks AI generated.
What looks AI generated:
- Overuse of rounded cards
- Eyebrow headlines: short text above a main headline or title
- A generic float/fade animation throughout the page
- Generic slogans like "supercharge your workflow", "seamless integration", "reimagining the future"
- Use of a Bento grid or content wrapped in uniform rounded rectangles

Please note that items above are not explicitly banned, but excessive use should be avoided.
If you can swap out the logo and text of your application with an entirely different brand and the design still looks ok, the layout is too generic.
