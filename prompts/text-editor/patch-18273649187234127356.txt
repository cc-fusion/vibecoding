Change the following: 
1. Only truncate with "..." on the bottom bar. Never truncate inline previews.
2. Imperial temperature defaults to Rankine. Default to Fahrenheit.
3. "3C to F" and "3K to C" doesn't inline or suggest anything.
4. Electronvolts seem to be acting weirdly:
These inline: "3 meV = ", 
These don't inline: "3 eV = ", "3 keV = " 
5. "2**14=" autocompletes as 16380. "10000+10001=" autocompletes as 20000. "2**1000" autocompletes 1072 with 253 zeros. Do not round like this, and use decimal.js for arbitrary precision.
6. Only suggest, but don't inline dates and phone numbers: "12/31/2026", "415-555-0132"
7. `2psi to Pa` works, but `2PSI to Pa` and `2psi to pa` doesn't. Units should be case insensitive only if all lowercase, all uppercase, or titlecase unless they conflict
Examples:
2mW is 2 milliwatts, 2MW is 2 megawatts. 2mw and 2Mw are invalid
5psi, 5PSI and 5Psi are both 5 pounds per square inch, combinations such as 5psI, 5pSi, 5PSi, 5pSI are invalid
